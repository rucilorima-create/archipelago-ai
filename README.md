## 💡 INSPIRATION
As a performance analyst managing operations for **four airlines inside a high-volume call center**, I live in the eye of the storm. When catastrophic system failures, global IT outages, or extreme weather ground fleets, the frontline reality is brutal: thousands of passengers are stranded in airport terminals for hours with no food, no communication, and no access to decent accommodation. At the same time, airline staff and analysts face absolute operational paralysis. 

We own arable land and agricultural assets, but when a crisis strikes, we lack the mental bandwidth to manage them. We are forced to juggle crushing airline call center shifts, demanding business school workloads, and our own entrepreneurial ventures. I built **Archipelago AI** out of sheer necessity: a system to provide an automated, step-by-step survival playbook for both the grounded airline operations room and the analyst's personal life. It answers the one burning question everyone asks when a global system fails: "What do I do next?"

---

## 🚀 WHAT IT DOES
Archipelago AI is a complete, multi-layered ecosystem that spans all four hackathon tracks to tackle complex global and personal disruptions:

*   **Coding & Agentic Engineering (Developer & Workflow Agents):** The core engine utilizes autonomous, self-healing code generation workflows. If an external API schema changes or a flight-status data endpoint drops during a crisis, our agentic engine writes, tests, and deploys its own code patches dynamically to ensure continuous operation of critical feeds.
*   **Best Apps & Agents (Multi-Agent Platform & Productivity Tools):** Tracks live data from multiple airport hubs using the **Tavily AI Search API**. When cascading groundings happen, it coordinates emergency logistics: parsing civil aviation passenger rights, managing food and accommodation supply lines, and generating legal and policy briefs to guide communication with regulatory bodies like the **Ministry of Civil Aviation**.
*   **Personal AI (Hyper-Personalized Virtual Assistant):** A high-performance productivity router built specifically for individuals juggling **work, business school, and corporate ventures**. It maps out an exact daily routine—dynamically shifting calendar blocks, managing academic deadlines, tracking water intake, and generating rapid gym circuits and high-protein diet regimes to keep the user physically resilient under high-stress conditions.
*   **Physical AI (Robotics, Hardware Integrations & Simulation):** Operates directly on physical agricultural assets. Deployed on **NVIDIA Jetson** edge modules, it uses computer vision to automate weed control, run real-time water monitoring sensors, and pilot simulated autonomous air drones to protect crops and ensure food security while the owner is stuck on a call center floor or in a university lecture.

## 🛠️ HOW WE BUILT IT & WHAT IT TOOK
We engineered a hyper-efficient, production-ready dual-engine architecture designed to run **entirely within the free \$25 Token Factory credits** granted by Nebius, proving that world-class AI orchestration doesn't require enterprise budgets.

*   **The Cloud Infrastructure:** Hosted entirely on the **Nebius AI Cloud**. To stay strictly within our **\$25 budget**, we built an intelligent model-tiering structure. Heavy structural reasoning, legal analysis, and architecture generation tasks are routed to flagship **NVIDIA Nemotron** instances, while rapid text processing and routine operations are handled by ultra-low-cost, high-speed open-source weights to maximize token mileage.
*   **The Physical AI Simulation:** Built using **NVIDIA Cosmos** and **Sonic** to develop a Reinforcement Learning (RL) environment where simulated cargo drones learn to navigate harsh maritime wind shears and complex continental topographies.
*   **The Smart Routing Pipeline:** Implemented an optimized tier-routing system where simple daily routines and gym logging run on fast, low-cost models via Nebius, while critical multi-hub failures trigger our flagship reasoning agents.

## 🌪️ CHALLENGES WE RAN INTO
The primary technical challenge was building a multi-agent system that could dynamically prioritize wildly divergent data streams. We had to ensure that when a sudden 10,000-passenger flight cancellation event occurred, the **Logistics Agent** could instantly lock down regional accommodation protocols and auto-generate Ministry communication strategies without dropping or delaying the automated farm water monitoring telemetry or the user's business school assignment reminders. Additionally, engineering this complex system to compile, run tests, and manage inference **entirely under a \$25 credit ceiling** required aggressive context window compression and rigorous token optimization.
